# Aumo — Brand Brief (for designer)

**Product:** Aumo, an autonomous treasury agent for stablecoins on X Layer.
**One-liner:** Give a stablecoin a job.
**What it does:** deposits USDT0, allocates into the best risk-adjusted tokenized real-world-asset
yield on X Layer, rebalances on its own inside strict guardrails, and proves every move onchain.

## Personality
Trustworthy, calm, precise. Institutional but modern. This is **an agent you hand money to**, so the
feeling is quiet confidence, not hype. Think a private bank's discipline with a software-native edge.

## Hard nos
- No rounded "AI SaaS" look, no neon-everything, no generic purple gradients.
- Not degen, not playful, not memey.

## Direction
- **Sharp / editorial.** Restrained palette, one confident accent over strong neutrals.
- **Dark-first** (finance / onchain), with a clean light mode.
- Strong typographic hierarchy: a display face for headlines + a **monospace for data, numbers, receipts**
  (receipts and provable state are core to the product, lean into that).
- Corners minimal/sharp, not pill-rounded.
- Motif ideas to explore (optional): "au" = gold/aurum; a vault or seal; a steward/keeper; dusk-calm.

## Deliverables
- Wordmark for **Aumo** (+ a small standalone mark/favicon).
- Color system: 1 accent + neutral ramp, light + dark.
- Type pairing: display + mono (name the exact fonts).
- Favicon / social avatar (for @aumo_fi) and an OG/share image.

## References for the bar
Ondo, Morpho, Kamino level of polish. Editorial-finance, not crypto-carnival.
