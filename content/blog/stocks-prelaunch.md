Aumo started with one idea: a stablecoin treasury that manages itself. An autonomous agent that senses the market, reasons about risk, and moves capital onchain without you having to babysit it. We are now extending that same engine to something people have wanted onchain for a long time. Tokenized stocks.

Soon you will be able to hold NVIDIA, Tesla, Apple, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, Google, and Coinbase inside Aumo, as tokenized equities on X Layer, with the same agent handling execution.

## What this is

Each stock is a tokenized share (an xStock) backed one to one by the real equity. You deposit a stablecoin, choose the stock you want exposure to, and Aumo's agent buys and holds that xStock for you onchain. Your position tracks the stock's price. When you want out, you redeem back to stablecoin.

One thing we will say plainly, because it matters. Stocks on Aumo is directional. Unlike the safe treasury, which chases low-risk yield, a stock position rises and falls with the stock. You are taking on market exposure on purpose. That is the point, and it is a different product from the yield vault.

## How it works

- Deposit a stablecoin and pick a stock.
- The agent routes your deposit into the tokenized share onchain, priced against a live market feed.
- It respects market hours. When the market is closed, the position holds and entries and exits pause, the same way the real stock stops trading.
- You can redeem to stablecoin whenever the market is open.

No brokerage account, no forms, no settlement wait. Just an onchain position your agent manages.

## Built the honest way

We are not going to pretend the hard parts do not exist. A few things we are being upfront about before launch.

- It is at risk. A stock can go down. Aumo shows you that clearly and asks you to acknowledge it before you deposit.
- Pricing. Until an enterprise price network covers equities on X Layer, Aumo runs its own price feed for stocks, and we disclose that on the product page. It is a deliberate first version, and the design lets us move to a decentralized feed later without you doing anything.
- Liquidity. Onchain stock liquidity on X Layer is still early. We would rather launch when the path to a clean fill exists than ship something that hands you a bad price.

## What is next

The contracts, the agent executor, and the pricing are built and tested. We are finishing the last pieces of onchain routing and liquidity before we open it up. Follow along, and we will let you know the moment Stocks goes live on Aumo.
