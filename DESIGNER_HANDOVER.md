# Aumo — Designer Handover

## The product in one breath
**Aumo** is an autonomous treasury agent for stablecoins on X Layer. You deposit USDT0, and
an AI agent puts it to work in tokenized real-world-asset (RWA) yield, rebalancing on its own
inside strict guardrails, and proving every move onchain. It is an agent you hand money to.

- **Tagline:** Give a stablecoin a job.
- **Descriptor:** Autonomous RWA-yield agent for stablecoins on X Layer.
- **Category:** AI × RWA (not a stablecoin issuer).
- **Audience:** crypto builders and treasuries who want yield on idle stablecoins without babysitting it.

## Personality
Quiet institutional confidence. Precise, trustworthy, calm. A private bank's discipline with a
software-native edge. Because people trust it with real money, it must feel **safe and exact**,
never hyped.

## Hard nos
- No rounded "AI SaaS" look. No neon-everything. No generic purple gradients. Not memey, not degen.
- Do **not** echo aumo.ai (an unrelated Brazilian enterprise-AI company). Own our own identity.

## Visual direction
- **Sharp / editorial.** One confident accent over a strong neutral ramp. Minimal, sharp corners.
- **Dark-first** (finance / onchain), with a clean light mode.
- Accent: designer's call. A restrained gold nod to "au" (aurum) or a cool ink/steel both fit;
  avoid anything loud.
- Type: strong hierarchy, a **display** face for headlines + a **monospace for data, numbers,
  receipts** (receipts and provable state are core to the product, lean into that).
- Optional motifs to explore: gold/aurum, a vault or seal, a steward/keeper, dusk-calm.

## Deliverables (in priority order)
**Batch 1 — needed first, to build the site:**
1. **Wordmark** "Aumo" — SVG, horizontal.
2. **Icon / mark** — SVG, square, legible from 32px to 512px.
3. **Color system** — hex values, light + dark (accent + neutral ramp + semantic states).
4. **Type pairing** — display + mono, **named and available as web fonts (Google Fonts preferred)**
   so it drops straight into the Next.js site.

**Batch 2 — for the account + launch:**
5. **X avatar** — 400×400 PNG (from the mark).
6. **X header** — 1500×500 PNG.
7. **OG / social share image** — 1200×630 PNG.
8. **Favicon** — 512×512 PNG + .ico.

**Batch 3 — nice to have:** a one-screen landing hero direction, and a short usage guide.
