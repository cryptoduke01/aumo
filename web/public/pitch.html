01The opportunity

Hundreds of billions in stablecoins sit idle, earning nothing.

People hold digital dollars for safety and speed, then leave them parked. Earning a real return on them means picking the right protocols, watching risk around the clock, and getting out before things break. It is a full-time job, and the ones who chase the highest rate are usually the ones who get burned.

There is no safe, hands-off way to earn on stablecoins. That is the gap.

03Why it wins

Most yield bots chase numbers. Aumo actually manages risk.
